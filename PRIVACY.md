# Privacy Policy — Pula Expense Tracker

_Last updated: 16 May 2026_

Pula Expense Tracker ("the app") is designed to respect your privacy.

## Information we collect

The app does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

All data you enter — expense amounts, categories, dates, notes and your
monthly budget — is stored **only on your device** using the device's local
storage. This data never leaves your phone and is not sent to us or to any
third party.

## Internet access

The app does not require an internet connection and does not communicate
with any external server.

## Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera,
microphone, photos, or any other sensitive device feature.

## Data deletion

You can permanently delete all data stored by the app at any time from the
**Settings** screen using the "Clear All Data" option. Uninstalling the app
also removes all of its data from your device.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new
"Last updated" date.

## Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy, contact the app developer
through the Google Play store listing.
